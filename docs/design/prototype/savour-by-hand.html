Savour by hand

A feel prototype for Artifacts's cabinet, drawers and quick-log. Paper and ink are approximated in CSS; haptics play as sound, a vibration where the browser allows, and a small ring where your finger is.

Collections · the turning cabinet

  • Swipe the cabinet to turn it. Each drawer clicks into place and eases open to show its best keepsakes.
  • Pull the front drawer down (or tap it) to open it.
  • Pinch (or All drawers) to see the whole chest. There, hold a drawer to carry it somewhere else, or drop it in the storeroom to put it away.
  • Flick hard (or Lucky dip) to spin the cabinet and pull out a keepsake you'd forgotten. Films has an anniversary card sticking out.

Inside a drawer · the riffle

  • Thumb down to flip through. Tabs show the order (No. 1, No. 2 …). Tap a tab or the right-hand rail to jump.
  • Tap the front keepsake to lift it out, then tap again to turn it over.
  • Spread (or pinch) tips the drawer out onto the table.

Savour · draw a keepsake

  • Hold “Savour something”, slide onto a blank and let go. Once you know where things are, just flick. A tap opens the same hand as a sheet.
  • The blank prints its best guess. Tear it off sideways for the next one, or search.
  • Rank it, relish it or date it, then hold the ā to seal. The stopwatch shows your time.

Atlas · spin & stamp

  • Spin the globe, aim the dashed ring and press Stamp. Double-tap a dot to stamp it directly.
  • Type to fly there. A continent chip gives you a strip of unstamped stamps to tap through quickly.
  • Unroll (or pinch) for the flat map.
